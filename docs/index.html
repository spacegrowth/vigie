What it does

Lives in your menu bar

A glance at what changed, without opening anything. Click a line to read it in full.

Stays out of your way

Notifications tell you who did what. No snippets, no noise, and quiet hours if you want them.

Reads without leaving

Diffs, review comments and conversations render in the app. GitHub is one click away when you need to act.

Yours alone

Everything is stored locally in SQLite. No account, no server, no telemetry — your token talks to GitHub and nothing else.

Several accounts

Watch repos across personal and work accounts at once, each with its own credentials in the keychain.

Careful with your budget

Polling is cached and shows what is left of GitHub's hourly allowance, so it never burns through it behind your back.
